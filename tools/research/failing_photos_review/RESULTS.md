# Focused review: original genuine marker-limit exceptions — 20 October 2026

## Result — previous global perspective screen is NOT VALID for exclusion

The prior exploratory "strict" and "ultra-strict" thresholds admitted 0/50 GMT, 24/112 No-Date and 0/124 Date images. This is **not validated evidence** that all GMT and Date images were angled. Do not use those exclusion flags to discard genuine references, adjust tolerances or calculate authenticity accuracy. The screen is superseded as an admission method by direct, blinded case/crown review plus validated independent pose diagnostics.

To return to the original research question, 24 source views of 21 known genuine watches in the earlier outlier set were independently fetched by recorded SHA256, made into full-watch case/crown contact sheets and inspected **without using the marker verdict to decide photo angle**.

| Photo-level observation | Count | Research disposition |
|---|---:|---|
| Visible off-axis side/crown/case geometry | 7 | Exclude from any pristine calibration subset (provisional human visual judgement) |
| Looks reasonably frontal | 6 | Retain for further edge/marker inspection, NOT automatically admit |
| Uncertain camera yaw or pitch | 7 | Withhold pending independent original/full-resolution confirmation |
| Original source hash mismatch | 4 | Photo unavailable; cannot classify pose or reject watch |

The **visually near-frontal** Date examples are Bob's SKU 193218, 188539, 190021 and 181036; despite their original triangle strict-limit outliers or uncertainty, angle alone does not explain them. Review edge coverage, lighting/glare, template bias and uncertainty separately.

Phillips GMT Pepsi 210072 and lot 75 show more apparent off-axis crown/case geometry and are suitable candidates for exclusion based on photographic evidence rather than on marker deviations. SwissWatchExpo No-Date stock 80219 is ambiguous, not proven clean or angled by a nearly circular dial.

Conclusions apply only to these selected outlier views; they **do not** estimate source or model-wide angle rates. Source crop variants of one photograph do not count as independent views. Original photo and genuine references remain unchanged, no threshold or Android/app code changed.

## Required safe next step

1. Use a **predeclared human angle-review rubric** (dial and bezel symmetry; crown-guard/case wall compared to same reference; roll vs yaw vs pitch), on matched failing and passing examples with blinded marker labels.
2. Accept candidate photo **only** when front-on geometry independently supported and marker-edge fit reliable; if uncertain, withhold without calling the marker defective.
3. Investigate the accepted-frontal but out-of-tolerance 12 triangles as **measurement/reference problems**; repeat on the original high-resolution images. No outlier removals based on results.
4. Later derive and validate numeric pose thresholds with high-confidence face-on and visibly oblique controls before screening the rest of the dataset. Keep original photos and exclusions immutable.
