## Original experiments (IMPORTANT: exploratory, manually seeded)
1. `reconstruct_bevel.py`, `annotate_bevel_confidence.py`, `bevel_probe.py`, `bevel_stability.py` plus `BEVEL_RECONSTRUCTION_README.md`: pairs bright-to-dark contrast transitions across 66 cross-sections of each approximate triangle edge, normal to user-seeded guide lines (roughly 13%-85% of each side); seeks both inside lume/bevel and outside bevel/dial transitions and uses along-side continuity. ±20% thickness prior and 1 native-pixel guide jitters caused ambiguous lines, especially on QC left edge. Genuine top 66/66 stable, left 23/66, right 34/66; QC top 64/66, left 0/66, right 66/66. Stability is NOT correctness.
2. `lume_edge_experiment.py`: bright luminous fill mask from manually seeded ROI. Thresholds tried QC 115,145,175 and genuine 110,132,153. Inner boundary relatively more repeatable than reflecting outer bevel but not flawless, especially top edges.
3. `lume_anchored_bevel_test.py`: preliminary luminous-outline dilation by hypothesised thickness and fitting a visible right edge. Revealed the guessed width could be much too narrow and the algorithm can mistake bevel facets.
4. `bevel_width_previews.py`: current promising version. Segment luminous interior; select a continuous visible outer-edge anchor on the RIGHT side; estimate width as an 85th-percentile of candidate outer-edge separation converted to a distance approximately normal to that side; infer candidate outer boundary by outward distance transform from luminous mask. Exploratory widths: QC ~5.7 ORIGINAL-image pixels; older genuine ~18.7 pixels due different image scales/resolutions. THESE NUMBERS ARE NOT PHYSICAL FACTORY SPECS OR GENERALISED COMPARABLE PIXEL SCORES. QC alternative preview widths 4.3px, 5.7px, 7.1px. The inference is simplistic: one width everywhere is WRONG near tip/corners, and observed outer metal may be rounded or have beveled changing facet width.
5. `example_bevel_side_by_side.png`: main preview, QC then older genuine, each as original, pink luminous boundary, cyan predicted outer metal and green visible right-side anchor evidence.
6. `example_bevel_qc_width_options.png`, `example_bevel_qc.png`, `example_bevel_genuine.png`: width sensitivity and enlarged previews.

## Core user insight
Human vision follows the narrow polished metal strip through weak outer contrast, leveraging inner luminous boundary, locally plausible thickness, and continuity. A short clear segment of the OUTER metal edge can measure or confirm thickness, not just seed a guessed constant. A wide credible thickness may be more accurate than narrower candidates caused by incomplete visibility, BUT never take a stray glare/shadow as proof of a larger width. Candidate selected width needs coherent spatial support, multiple candidate anchors if available, and independent held-out visible outer sections for validation. Let thickness vary by facet/side only with actual evidence. The outer metal near rounded points/corners does NOT remain parallel to the luminous triangle. The perfect physical bevel shape can be researched later; DO NOT invent precision at apex or corners now.
