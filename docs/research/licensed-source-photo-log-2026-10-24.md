## Correction to the 2026-10-23 report

The prior report read the Watches of Switzerland and Goldsmiths retail terms too broadly. Their retail terms explicitly allow downloading or printing site content for the user's own personal use, subject to the terms. This allows a private, non-commercial research copy in the owner's personal archive, with accurate reproduction. If content is reproduced or issued to others, source and copyright status must be acknowledged. Other use, including publication, distribution, alteration, or other exploitation, requires written permission. The earlier conclusion that no CPO image could be retained for private research is superseded.

This personal-use permission does **not** authorize redistribution, public GitHub image storage, a distributed Android app, or commercial calibration/reference datasets. These six files are staged only as private, personal-use research candidates. They are not admitted to the production or public Suitable Photos collection and are not automatically eligible for calibration. Yacht-Master is also outside the current selection schema.

Sources: [Watches of Switzerland UK retail terms](https://www.watches-of-switzerland.co.uk/terms-and-conditions); [Goldsmiths retail terms](https://www.goldsmiths.co.uk/terms-and-conditions). Source images remain credited and linked to the relevant listing below. This log publishes metadata and hashes only, not photographs.

### Provenance and independence

All six listings identify the watches as Rolex Certified Pre-Owned and state that the CPO guarantee card confirms genuineness at sale. This is strong documented retailer/CPO provenance, not authentication inferred from the pixels alone. The three GMT items, one Submariner item, and two Yacht-Master items have separate inventory codes. Those codes support six distinct offered stock items, but no serial-number or service-document cross-check was available, so the physical-watch independence claim remains listing-level rather than serial-level proof.

## Exclusions and duplicate control

- Goldsmiths Submariner Date 126610LV item 406105552490 was visually compared to the retained 126610LV image. It is a near-duplicate style and unusually close in image similarity (perceptual hash distance: 4 bits); only one was retained to avoid inflating the evidence count. Its inspected image hash was `d89ce8b4b1622f237f217eff5ae4bf3f35f6c17e385ae50392c2c0baf0eacbba`. This exclusion does not establish that the underlying watches were the same physical watch.
- Two openly licensed Wikimedia GMT photographs were rejected as genuine evidence because their source descriptions identify them as replicas: [Batgirl replica](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rolex-GMT-Master-II-Batgirl.jpg) and [126710BLRO replica](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rolex_GMT_Master_II_126710BLRO.jpg).
- Older GMT references and unrelated Yacht-Master references found in open-licence searches do not fill these exact reference gaps. Official Rolex catalogue imagery is model identification material, not independent physical-watch evidence, and was not retained.
