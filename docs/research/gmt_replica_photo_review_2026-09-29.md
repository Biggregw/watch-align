# Replica photo review (2026-09-29)

The genuine rules (face-on with the 12 up, sharp, no hand over the 12/6/9, at most one
round marker covered, no watermark over the dial, one photo per watch) were applied by
eye to the replica photos.

## Existing replica set (210 photos, 32 watches)

Only 3 watches have a photo that passes: rep_cf_6I00d8w, rep_cplus_wEYZOyK and
rep_vsf_p3hHVMB (`gmt_replica_clean_set_2026-09-29.csv`). All three are 126710BLNR, and
the dial is 350-450 px across, well under the genuine set.

The rest fail on:
- the minute or hour hand lying over the 9, or the minute hand at the 12 (seller QC photos
  are often taken at 9:xx or on the hour): most watches;
- side, clasp, caseback, movement or timegrapher shots;
- angled or hand-held shots;
- a guide overlay drawn on the dial (rep_cf_xK08a3L image_00, rep_arf_8xahrjm image_00).

The user's ARF Pepsi and ONE date-4 photos also have a hand touching two round markers.
They are left out under the same rule, not because of the photo quality.

## TheOneWatches dealer photos (84 products, 20 factories, not used)

TheOneWatches lists every current GMT by factory, with about 40 photos of the actual
replica per product. The second photo of each is a near face-on dial close-up (1300 x 860,
dial about 700 px). The app read the dial on 70 of 84, and flagged the 12 gap on about 30.

These aren't used, because every photo has a watermark across the dial:
- red "TheOneWatches" text at 3-9 height, over the 9 baton;
- grey "@theonewatches_steve" text around the dial, running through the gap between the
  minute track and the top of the 12 triangle, which is where the 12 gap is measured.

So it can't be told whether the 12 gap flags are real. One product (19277, VSF BLNR) only
had a photo labelled "Gen Pic": the dealer used a genuine watch's photo.

Reddit (RepTime QC posts) and the Replica Watch Info forum's image host are blocked from
this environment.

## RepTimeQC albums found by ChatGPT (2026-09-29)

24 Imgur albums were checked (the Reddit, Yupoo, Mega and imgbox links can't be fetched
here). 5 photos pass: Clean and ARF 126710GRNR, VSF 126720VTNR, VSF and ARF 126710BLRO.
The others failed mainly on a hand over the 9, 12, 6 or (Sprite) 3, an angled shot, or a QC
overlay drawn on the dial. One album listed as a VSF Pepsi (qOu9YiZ) is a Sprite.

Observation, not a result: the 12 gap on several of these replicas reads 0.065-0.075
(VSF Pepsi 0.068, ARF Pepsi 0.074, VSF Sprite 0.038 and 0.065), under every clean genuine
photo (0.084-0.105). Most of these readings are withheld by the app's own checks, so they
are not judged; the VSF Sprite at 0.038 is flagged CHECK.

### Third batch (23 Imgur albums)

11 photos pass: 6 x 126720VTNR (5 Clean, 1 VSF), 2 x 126711CHNR (Clean, GMF), 3 x
126710BLRO (VSF, Clean, GMF). Two albums were mislabelled: AprFyb6 is a Submariner and
sC9b4ad is a Pepsi, not a Sprite. The clean replica set is now 19 photos.

Both VSF Sprites from different posts (grmFo4g, iHuZ4gx) get a 12 gap CHECK, reading 0.038
and 0.028; the genuine clean photos read 0.084-0.105. The Clean Sprites read 0.070-0.101.
EFgYxw5 (0.200) and lOJmEYb (0.161) are withheld by the app and look like misfits.
