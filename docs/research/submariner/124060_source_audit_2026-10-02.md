# 124060 genuine-source audit — 2026-10-02

Purpose: fix the calibration-input problem exposed by alpha75/alpha76. The previous accepted provisional calibration was dominated by Bob's Watches photography. This audit identifies dealer-owned listing/category pages that can supply independent genuine 124060 watches without search-engine scraping or bypassing bot protection.

## Calibration rule

Image count alone is not adequate evidence. A fresh 124060 calibration run must fail before fitting limits unless its **acquired genuine watches** meet the configured source-diversity requirements. Discovery hits do not count. This prevents one dealer with many listings from satisfying the gate while other configured sources fail acquisition.

The split must also preserve source identity for genuine watches so development/validation/holdout are not accidentally treated as one undifferentiated `Rolex` stratum.

This change only improves acquisition and calibration-input governance. It does not alter Android QC thresholds, current verdicts, detector geometry or GMT behaviour.
