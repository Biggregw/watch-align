## Change control

**Any future change to a tolerance, allowance or reliability rule below needs the owner's explicit approval.** It must be
recorded in this file, or in a new dated baseline that supersedes it, with the old value, the new value, the date, the
reason and the commit. Tests pin the frozen values (`WatchClassificationTest.theFrozenLimitsAreUnchanged`). Do not
recalculate, optimise or re-derive them from new photos without that approval.

Units: **% R** is percent of the dial radius (the canonical dial radius is 1; a value in source code `x` R is `100·x` % R).
Angles are in degrees. *px* means pixels of the uploaded photo.

## 4. Resolution allowance and resolution rules

- **Allowance.** 0.5 px (`ClearlyOff.LOWRES_ALLOWANCE_PX=0.5`), applied once per photo as `100 · 0.5 / dial radius px`
  % R. It is added to every position, sideways and in/out maximum and subtracted from the gap minimum, in both
  standards. It is not applied to rotations.
- **Low resolution.** A dial radius under 200 px (`ClearlyOff.LOW_RES_RPX = AlignmentDecision.MIN_DIAL_RADIUS_PX=200`)
  makes every outer-metal reading *uncertain*. The reading is still measured, and the one-side refit is not used.
- **Lume path minimum.** `ClearlyOff.MIN_RPX=150` (lume path only).
- **Triangle rotation readings.** These are withheld below a 200 px dial radius (`ROT_MIN_RPX`), when an edge column
  lacks contrast (`ROT_MIN_CONTRAST=30`), when the edge fit is rough (`ROT_MAX_RMS_PX=0.35`), or when the two half-edge
  fits disagree (`ROT_MAX_HALF_DIFF_DEG=0.6`).

## 5. Measurement reliability rules (outer-metal fit)

Source: `MetalFit.java` and `WatchClassification.java`.

**Can't check (no measurement).** Any one of these:
- fewer than 24 edge points (`MIN_POINTS`);
- position not fixed to 1 % R (`CANT_SE_POS=0.01`) or rotation not fixed to 1° (`CANT_SE_ROT=1.0`);
- a hand confirmed by the per-marker check hiding at least half the outline (`HAND_HIDDEN_CANT=0.5`) with under half
  of it seen (`MIN_COVER=0.5`);
- any gate (hand check, no overlay or reference);
- a photo-wide reason (dial or pose not usable).

**Uncertain** (measured, with a warning). Any one of these:
- RMS over 0.5 px (`EDGE_RMS_OK`);
- more than 15 % of the seen edge inconsistent, where a point is inconsistent when its residual exceeds 1.0 px
  (`EDGE_BAD_FRAC=0.15`, `EDGE_BAD_PX=1.0`);
- under 50 % of the outline seen (`MIN_COVER`);
- position standard error over 0.25 % R (`MAX_SE_POS=0.0025`), or rotation standard error over 0.35°
  (`MAX_SE_ROT=0.35`);
- a dial radius under 200 px.

**Reliable** (`WatchClassification.reliable`): measured, nothing withheld, not uncertain, and for the triangle its gap
measured rather than placed from the outline (`gapFromFit`). **Only reliable readings can pass the genuine standard.**

**Credible** (`WatchClassification.credible`, owner 21–22 Oct 2026): a reading that may decide a **failure**. Every
reliable reading is credible. An uncertain reading is credible too, **however rough or inconsistent its edge fit**: no
RMS, inconsistency or coverage threshold applies. It is **not** credible when any of these apply:
- it is Can't check or gated;
- it has no finite measurement;
- the per-marker check confirmed a hand at it (the one-side refit below decides those).

**Drawn guide lines** (`GuideLines`, owner 21 Oct 2026): before the per-marker hand / glare check, thin, long,
strongly coloured lines of a single hue drawn on the photo are painted out of a copy that only the hand check reads.
This is detection, not a tolerance. The marker measurements still read the photo itself. Coloured hands, bezels and
date discs are never removed.

A photo-wide failure decides first (section 6). A triangle gap placed from the outline (`gapFromFit`) is not a measured
gap and never counts as a failure.

**One-side refit beside a confirmed hand** (batons, strict genuine standard only; `MetalFit` and `WatchClassification.alt`):
- **When it runs:** the baton's fit is uncertain, the per-marker check confirmed a hand, and the hand line runs along
  exactly one side (within 15°, `ALT_HAND_PARALLEL_DEG`, and 0.02 R of its midpoint, `ALT_HAND_NEAR_R`).
- **When it is kept:**
  - what remains is clean by the uncertain-fit tests above;
  - the dial radius is at least 200 px;
  - the marker's own reading is within replica QC;
  - the luminous centre agrees across the left-out side within 0.30 % R (`ALT_LUME_AGREE_R=0.0030`).
- **How it is judged,** with U<sub>pos</sub> = 0.65 % R (`ALT_U_POS_R=0.0065`) and U<sub>rot</sub> = 0.80°
  (`ALT_U_ROT_DEG=0.80`):
  - pass when |value| + U is inside both strict limits;
  - fail when |value| − U is outside one;
  - otherwise undecided.
- **Origin:** set on 20 Oct 2026 from the verified genuine photos
  (`tools/research/shared_engine/REFIT_BESIDE_HAND.md`).

## 6. Whole-watch classification rules

Source: `WatchClassification.classify`. The rules are applied in this order:

1. **Can't Determine** if there is a photo-wide failure: the analysis failed, or the dial or pose is not usable.
2. **Replica — QC Issues** if any credible reading is over a replica-QC limit (section 3), even when other markers
   cannot be assessed.
3. **Replica** otherwise, if any credible reading is outside a strict genuine limit (section 1), or a validated refit
   fails. One marker is enough, even when other markers cannot be assessed.
4. **Can't Determine** otherwise, if any required marker is missing, or is neither reliable nor decided by a validated
   refit.
5. **Genuine** when every required marker is reliable (or passed by a validated refit) and within every strict genuine
   limit.

- **Required markers:** 12 for Submariner 124060; 11 for GMT-Master II 126710 and Submariner Date 126610. The date
  window is not a marker.
- **Partial coverage:** a Replica or Replica — QC Issues result with some markers not assessed shows "Some markers could
  not be assessed on this photo, so additional QC issues may exist."
- **Fixed rules:** no averaging, no counting of passing markers, no confidence percentage.
- **Disclaimer:** "Genuine" and "Replica" are alignment-based screening predictions, never proof of authenticity, and
  the screen always shows the disclaimer.
- **Green ticks** on markers mean within replica QC; they do not mean genuine.
